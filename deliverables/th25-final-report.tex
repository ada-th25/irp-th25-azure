\begin{abstract}
Wildfires are destructive and a rapidly evolving hazard. With climate change, they become increasingly severe and frequent. This increases the need for a better wildfire risk prediction and monitoring using satellite data. This project aims to achieve a high-resolution spatial downscaling of global wildfire data.

\end{abstract}
